# Day 12 – Build Your Complete Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit

## Overview

On Day 12, I used Claude AI to create a complete Job Search & Personal Branding Toolkit. The objective was to analyze my resume, compare it with a Data Science job description, identify skill gaps, improve ATS compatibility, and build a roadmap for career growth.

---

## What I Did

### 1. Uploaded My Resume

I uploaded my latest resume to Claude AI and selected **Data Science Intern** as my target role.

### 2. Added a Job Description

I provided a Data Science Intern job description containing skills such as Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Statistics, Data Visualization, GitHub, and Power BI.

### 3. Generated a Career Toolkit

Claude analyzed both the resume and job description and generated a complete career growth toolkit.

---

## ATS Resume Analysis

The ATS review highlighted several areas for improvement:

* Add more industry-specific keywords.
* Quantify project achievements.
