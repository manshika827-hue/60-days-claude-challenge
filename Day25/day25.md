# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator 🦈

## 📌 Task Completed
Built a complete **AI Shark Tank Simulator** as a single HTML application where users can pitch startup ideas and get evaluated by AI judges.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The application simulates a real **Shark Tank-style pitch environment** where:

- Users submit a startup idea
- 4 AI judges evaluate the idea
- Each judge asks questions
- User responds in real-time
- System calculates final investment score
- Decision is generated automatically

---

## 🧠 AI Judges

1. 🦈 Venture Capitalist  
   - Focus: Market size, scalability, growth potential  

2. 🦈 Founder Judge  
   - Focus: Execution ability, practicality  

3. 🦈 Customer Judge  
   - Focus: Usefulness and user value  

4. 🦈 Angel Investor  
   - Focus: Profitability and early returns  

---

## 📊 Scoring System

The startup is evaluated on:

- Market Potential
- Innovation
- Business Model
- Execution
- Investment Worthiness

Final score is calculated out of **100**.

---

## 💰 Investment Decision Logic

Based on score:

- **80–100 → Invest**
- **60–79 → Come Back Later**
- **40–59 → Reject**
- **90+ → Acquisition Opportunity**

Also generates:
- Suggested valuation
- Funding amount
- Reasoning summary
